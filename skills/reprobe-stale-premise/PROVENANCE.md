# Structured probe helper provenance

The optional helper, its tests and its contract/runbook are independently authored
for this repository under the [Orion MIT license](LICENSE.orion). They implement
bounded local observations, rather than importing or redistributing an internal
goal launcher, shell executor, private workflow or operator-state integration.

Internal recon material was inspected only as design evidence. Its redistribution
basis was not confirmed; no implementation or prose from it is included in this
extension. The existing manual skill procedure is retained. This statement does
not resolve the separate research implementation's provenance question or lift
the collection release hold.

Retain the complete MIT notice, helper and linked documentation in standalone
copies. Local helper tests do not establish native-host qualification, change the
existing qualified starter set or its installer pin, or authorize installation.
